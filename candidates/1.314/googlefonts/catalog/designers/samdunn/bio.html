Sam Dunn is a product designer and entrepreneur, and the designer of Sunny Marker. His work brings together visual design, software, and practical tools for everyday use. Sunny Marker began as an exploration of friendly handwritten lettering for Sunny, with small annotations, labels, and playful interfaces in mind. The family carries that character across a variable range of weights, widths, and optical sizes, from compact notes to generous headings. Sam shares the typeface and its editable sources under the SIL Open Font License so others can use it, study it, and contribute to its development.
