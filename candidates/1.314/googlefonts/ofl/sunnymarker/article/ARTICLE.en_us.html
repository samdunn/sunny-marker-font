Sunny Marker is a flexible handwritten marker typeface for labels, notes, headings, and playful interfaces. Its rounded strokes retain the uneven rhythm of marker lettering across weight, width, slant, and optical size.

A small annotation can point to a detail, add a reminder, or bring a warmer voice to a familiar interface. Sunny Marker supports those little moments as well as larger headlines. The family moves from condensed to expanded widths, with upright lettering and a gentle nine-degree italic. Weight ranges from Light to Bold, while optical sizing opens spacing and small counters for use at smaller sizes.

Designed by Sam Dunn, the family includes Latin accents, punctuation, currencies, directional arrows, and a small collection of playful symbols. The project provides editable UFO sources and a reproducible build pipeline under the SIL Open Font License. Development includes AI-assisted concept work and font engineering.

To contribute, see the Sunny Marker project on GitHub.
